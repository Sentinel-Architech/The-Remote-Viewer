Buyer steps

  1. Scan a QR or tap Pay with Phantom (exact memo is pre-filled). Save the transaction signature.
  2. On your device only: 
    age-keygen -o identity.txt
    Keep AGE-SECRET-KEY private. Copy only the age1… public key.
  3. Preferred: open show-age1.html, paste your age1…, show the QR. Seller scans it.
    Fallback: send age1… + tx signature by any channel you already use with the seller.
  4. Receive a .trvl file (or optical frames).
  5. Decrypt locally: 
    cat sale.trvl | bash buyer-receive.sh identity.txt

If you pay before the seller has your age1, delivery stays PENDING.
