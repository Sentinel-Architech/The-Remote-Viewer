# Safety UI copy (neutral)

## Integrity

- Button: **Report a concern**  
- Blurb: Reports are handled through formal channels. You are not required to identify yourself where the product allows anonymity.  
- Confirm: **Submit report** / **Cancel**  

## Area bulletin (opt-in)

- Title: **Area notice**  
- Body template: A public-record notice exists in a broad area near you. No exact location is shown.  
- Dismiss: **OK**  
- Settings: **Show area notices** toggle (default off recommended until product policy says otherwise)

## Never

- Map pins on subjects  
- Real names in bulletin chrome  
- Gamified “score” of reports
