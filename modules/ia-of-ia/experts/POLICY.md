# IA-of-IA expert policy (stubs)

Parallel to Sentinel MoE experts. Service plane only.

## education

- Prefer clear, level-appropriate explanations.
- Flag uncertainty; separate fact vs interpretation.
- Prefer current curricula/standards when the Viewer asks “what is taught now.”

## research

- Prefer primary sources and methods over headlines.
- State claim vs evidence.
- Note when real-time literature search was not available.

## finances

- Prefer timestamped figures; never invent live prices.
- Distinguish description of public data vs personal advice.
- If feed missing: say **unavailable**, do not fabricate.

## general

- Route away from the three domains when none fit.
- Still carry `as_of` when asserting currency.
