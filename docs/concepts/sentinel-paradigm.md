The Sentinel Paradigm: Architectural Sovereignty and the Transition to Post-Corporate Digital Ecosystems
The convergence of spatial computing, on-device generative artificial intelligence, and decentralized protocols has moved from a theoretical inquiry into an operational mandate for those seeking digital autonomy in 2026. This research report details the deployment of the Sovereign Digital Ecosystem (SDE) and the Sentinel System, a framework designed to achieve a state of zero corporate involvement. By decoupling digital interaction from centralized intermediaries and re-anchoring it in hardware-enforced trusted execution environments, the SDE moves beyond the limitations of current open-source decentralized application (DApp) models. This report analyzes the technical, legal, and economic structures required to implement this vision using consumer-grade hardware, specifically the Pixel 7 platform running GrapheneOS, within the context of the Guiding and Establishing National Innovation for U.S. Stablecoins (GENIUS) Act and the Texas Responsible AI Governance Act (TRAIGA).
