# Vault Principles (Locked)

**Status:** Locked — July 25, 2026

## Primary Motto
**YOU CHOOSE TO BURN FOR YOUR PROTECTION**

## Supporting Rule
**Destroy = Restart from Square One**

## Meaning
The Vault is the sealed, user-controlled space for private data, journals, credentials, and sensitive materials.

When a Viewer elects permanent destruction of their Vault contents and associated identity path:
- The action is voluntary and deliberate
- The destruction is complete and irreversible
- All status, history, credentials, and data associated with that identity path are permanently erased
- Any future return begins as a completely new Viewer with zero continuity

## Design Intent
This is the final expression of sovereignty.  
The Viewer holds the absolute right to erase everything and walk away clean.

The motto and rule exist to make that right transparent, final, and protective.
