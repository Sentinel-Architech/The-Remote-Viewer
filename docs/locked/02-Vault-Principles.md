# Vault Principles (Locked)

**Status:** Locked — July 25, 2026  
**Classification:** Non-negotiable

---

## Primary Motto

**YOU CHOOSE TO BURN FOR YOUR PROTECTION**

## Supporting Rule

**Destroy = Restart from Square One**

---

## What the Vault Is

The Vault is the sealed, user-controlled space inside The Remote Viewer for:

- Private journals and personal records
- Sensitive credentials and keys
- Health or medical notes the user chooses to keep
- Any material the Viewer considers private

It is designed as a digital equivalent of a physical safe that only the owner can open — and that the owner can choose to destroy completely.

---

## Meaning of the Motto

“YOU CHOOSE TO BURN FOR YOUR PROTECTION” is deliberately direct.

- **YOU CHOOSE** — The decision is voluntary and deliberate. The platform never forces destruction.
- **TO BURN** — Destruction is total. There is no soft delete, no archive, no recoverable backup held by the platform.
- **FOR YOUR PROTECTION** — The purpose of the burn is the Viewer’s own safety and privacy, not the convenience of the system.

This language was refined for legal clarity and white-hat alignment. It emphasizes agency and benefit to the individual rather than any punitive or platform-centric framing.

---

## What Happens When a Viewer Chooses to Burn

When permanent destruction is elected and confirmed:

1. The action is treated as final and irreversible.
2. All Vault contents associated with that identity path are cryptographically destroyed or rendered permanently inaccessible.
3. All status, history, credentials, membership progress, journal content, and prior identity linkages are erased.
4. Any future return to the platform begins as a completely new Viewer with zero continuity.

There is no “recover my old account” path after a confirmed burn. This is intentional.

---

## Design Intent

This is the final expression of sovereignty.

Most platforms offer deletion that is incomplete, delayed, or still leaves residual records for legal, analytical, or recovery reasons. The Remote Viewer rejects that model. The Viewer holds the absolute right to erase everything and walk away clean.

The motto and the supporting rule exist to make that right:

- **Transparent** — the user knows exactly what will happen
- **Final** — no hidden retention
- **Protective** — the burn exists for the user’s benefit, not the platform’s

---

## Interaction with Other Locked Principles

- **Identity Layer** — Credentials and identity attestations stored in or linked to the Vault are destroyed along with it.
- **Destroy = Restart** — The Vault burn is the concrete mechanism that enforces the absolute restart rule.
- **Founding Sovereign Viewer & Membership** — These statuses do not survive a confirmed burn. They are tied to the identity path that is being erased.

---

## Operational Notes

- Destruction requires clear, confirmed user intent (no accidental one-click deletion).
- The system may provide a short cooling-off or confirmation window, but once the final command is accepted, the process is irreversible.
- No platform employee, partner, or automated system retains the ability to restore a burned Vault.
