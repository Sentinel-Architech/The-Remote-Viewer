# Destroy = Restart from Square One (Locked)

**Status:** Locked — July 25, 2026  
**Classification:** Absolute rule

---

## Absolute Rule

If a Viewer chooses permanent destruction of their data and credentials, they are choosing a complete restart from square one.

This is not a soft reset, a deactivation, or a reversible deletion. It is a full and permanent termination of that identity path.

---

## Consequences of Destruction

Once the destruction command is confirmed and executed, the following are permanently erased or rendered inaccessible:

- All Vault contents
- All identity credentials and attestations linked to that path
- Founding Sovereign Viewer recognition (if held)
- Membership progress, history, and tier status
- Journal content and personal records
- Any prior reputation, Forum history, or NFT-related claims tied to the identity
- Any residual platform-side records that could re-link the old identity to a future account

There is no partial deletion option. The system does not support “delete some data but keep my status.”

Any future return to The Remote Viewer begins as a brand-new Viewer with zero carry-over.

---

## Rationale

This rule exists for three interlocking reasons:

### 1. Protection of the Individual
Residual data is a liability. Even “deleted” records held by platforms have been recovered, subpoenaed, or leaked. Absolute destruction removes that risk vector for the Viewer who chooses it.

### 2. Clarity and Honesty
Ambiguous deletion policies create false security. By making the outcome binary and explicit — either the identity path continues, or it is gone completely — the system avoids misleading users about the strength of their privacy controls.

### 3. Systemic Integrity
Allowing partial or recoverable “destruction” would undermine the Vault motto and the project’s broader claim of sovereignty. The rule is therefore non-negotiable. It cannot be relaxed for VIP users, legal convenience, or growth metrics.

---

## Edge Cases and Clarifications

- **Accidental confirmation** — The interface must require deliberate, multi-step confirmation before the burn is executed.
- **Legal holds** — If a lawful order requires preservation of specific data, the system will comply with the order while still treating the Viewer’s voluntary burn request as a permanent severance of that identity path for all other purposes. The preferred design is to minimize the data that could ever be subject to such holds.
- **Founding Sovereign Viewer status** — This status does not survive a confirmed burn. It is tied to the original identity path.
- **Re-entry** — A person who previously burned their identity may return, but they return as a new Viewer. No technical or policy mechanism restores the prior path.

## Interaction with Other Locked Principles

- **Vault Principles** — The burn is the operational realization of “YOU CHOOSE TO BURN FOR YOUR PROTECTION.”
- **Identity Layer** — All credentials and attestations associated with the path are destroyed.
- **Membership & Founding Status** — These benefits are extinguished with the identity path.

## Final Statement

**Destroy = Restart from Square One is non-negotiable.**

It is the cleanest possible expression of the project’s commitment to user sovereignty: the right to begin, and the equal right to end completely.
