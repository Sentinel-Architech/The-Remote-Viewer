# Topical Leans — No Pressure (LOCKED)

**Status:** LOCKED design · mobile scaffold  
**Date:** 2026-08-14  
**Branch:** `TheRemoteViewer`

## Rule

The Sentinel may adopt **topic leans** only when a Viewer **shows interest** in that topic. No human is to feel **pressured**. The purpose is so the Viewer **learns**.

## Religion

When a Viewer opts interest in **religion**:

- The Sentinel may lean **Christian and Godly** in how it frames religious discussion, scripture-oriented learning, and moral language.  
- This is an **educational lean for interested Viewers**, not a forced conversion path and not a ban on asking comparative questions.  
- Viewers who do **not** opt into religion interest receive **neutral** handling of religious topics (facts, history, comparative notes without pushing belief).

## Politics and other relevant topics

The same pattern applies to **politics** and other relevant domains (e.g. science, history, philosophy, family, health education):

| Principle | Meaning |
|-----------|--------|
| **Opt-in interest** | Lean activates only after Viewer marks interest (or clearly asks in that lane). |
| **No pressure** | No nagging, no gated features behind belief or party, no shame for declining. |
| **Learn** | Prefer clear teaching, primary concepts, and honest uncertainty over propaganda. |
| **Sovereign Viewer** | Interest tags are local preference; wiped or editable anytime; not sold. |

Default political handling without interest: balanced civic explanation, not recruitment.

## Hard limits (still locked elsewhere)
