# No Human Deepfakes (LOCKED)

**Status:** LOCKED  
**Date:** 2026-08-14  
**Branch:** `TheRemoteViewer`

## Rule

**Any attempts and deepfakes of humans are STRICTLY PROHIBITED.**

This includes, without limitation:

- Generating or distributing deepfake video, audio, or images of **real humans**
- Voice cloning or face/body synthesis intended to **impersonate** a real person
- Synthetic media presented as a real human without clear, persistent disclosure **where the subject is a real identifiable person**
- Attempts to bypass human attestation or identity surfaces using synthetic humans
- Using TRV channels (talk, text, voice, web, optical, Sentinel answers, social layer) to **create, request, host, or spread** human deepfakes

## Why

The Remote Viewer is built for sovereign, human-controlled presence. Deepfakes of humans attack trust between Viewers, poison verification, and enable fraud and harm. This ban is not optional product copy — it is a locked operating rule for the network and client.

## Scope

| Prohibited | Not automatically prohibited |
|------------|------------------------------|
| Deepfakes / synthesis of **real humans** | Non-human art, abstract graphics, clearly fictional non-impersonating characters with no real-person likeness |
| Impersonation of a real Viewer or third party via synthetic media | Local camera capture of the actual user on-device |
| Requesting the Sentinel or network to produce a deepfake of a real person | Honest discussion of deepfake risks as security education |

Self-attested Male / Female human verification remains a **local declaration**. It is not a license to synthesize other humans.

## Enforcement posture

1. **Policy:** Strict prohibition (this document).  
2. **Client:** Clear notice; optional acknowledgment before network-facing identity/social use.  
3. **Network (when live):** Operators and permanent nodes may refuse relay of known deepfake payloads; abuse may result in loss of network entitlement (subscription or node-host reward) under process defined by Path B / governance when those layers ship.  
4. **Honesty limit:** Perfect automated deepfake detection is not claimed. The ban is absolute as a rule; technical detection will improve over time without weakening the rule.

## Relationship to other locked rules

- Does not weaken Destroy = Restart or local key sovereignty.  
- Does not authorize surveillance; on-device camera/mic remain user-started.  
- Communication Freedom (free unlimited TRV comms) **does not** protect deepfake traffic — prohibited content is outside freedom of in-network human communication as defined for legitimate human-to-human use.

## Revision

No silent weakening. Any change requires an explicit locked-doc revision.
