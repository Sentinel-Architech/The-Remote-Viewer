# No Human Deepfakes (LOCKED)

**Status:** LOCKED  
**Date:** 2026-08-14 (distinguishability clarification)  
**Branch:** `TheRemoteViewer`

## Rule

**Deepfakes and attempts that present synthetic media as a real human — or that are not clearly distinguishable from humanity — are STRICTLY PROHIBITED.**

**Human likeness is allowed only when it is distinguishable from humanity.**

| Allowed | Strictly prohibited |
|---------|---------------------|
| Stylized, artistic, or clearly synthetic figures **in the likeness of a human** that a reasonable Viewer can tell are **not** a real person | Deepfakes of **real** people |
| Fictional characters, avatars, or mascots that do not pass as live humans | Voice/face/body synthesis used to **impersonate** a real person |
| Abstract or exaggerated humanoid forms with obvious non-real cues | Synthetic media of a human that is **indistinguishable** from a real human (or designed to be) |
| Education about deepfake risks | Bypassing human attestation or identity using passable synthetic humans |
| Local camera capture of the **actual** Viewer on-device | Creating, requesting, hosting, or spreading passable human deepfakes on TRV rails |

## Distinguishability standard

A human-like depiction is **OK** only if it is **distinguishable from humanity** — e.g. obvious stylization, labels, non-photoreal failure cues, or other signals that prevent a reasonable person from treating it as a real human being.

If it could pass as a real person in ordinary use, it is **not** allowed under this rule.

## Why

The Remote Viewer protects trust between humans. Passable deepfakes attack that trust. Clearly non-real human *likeness* (art, avatars, fiction) does not require the same ban — provided it never pretends to be, or blurs into, real humanity.

## Scope on TRV channels

Applies to talk, text, voice, web, optical, Sentinel surfaces, social layer, and any future TRV human-communication channel.

Communication Freedom (free unlimited TRV comms) **does not** protect prohibited deepfake / indistinguishable-human synthetic traffic.

## Enforcement posture

1. **Policy:** Strict ban on passable / impersonating deepfakes; allow distinguishable human likeness.  
2. **Client:** Notice + acknowledgment.  
3. **Network (when live):** Nodes may refuse relay of prohibited payloads; abuse may cost entitlement under Path B process when shipped.  
4. **Honesty:** Perfect automated detection is not claimed. The rule is absolute; detectors may improve later.

## Revision

No silent weakening of the ban on indistinguishable or real-person deepfakes. Distinguishability language is intentional and locked.
