# No Human Deepfakes (LOCKED)

**Status:** LOCKED  
**Date:** 2026-08-14 (animation + distinguishability)  
**Branch:** `TheRemoteViewer`  
**Related:** `17-Conduct-Community-IA.md`

## Rule

**Deepfakes and attempts that present synthetic media as a real human — or that are not clearly distinguishable from humanity — are STRICTLY PROHIBITED.**

**Human likeness is allowed only when it is distinguishable from humanity.**

**Any animation made of a human is OK** when it stays distinguishable (see also locked doc 17). Adult animation is OK only behind **XXX blocking**.

| Allowed | Strictly prohibited |
|---------|---------------------|
| Stylized / animated / clearly synthetic human likeness | Deepfakes of **real** people |
| Humanoid animation that cannot pass as a live person | Synthesis designed to be **indistinguishable** from a real human |
| Adult animation **with XXX mark**, viewed only by XXX opt-in | Impersonation of a real Viewer or third party |
| Local capture of the **actual** Viewer | Unmarked adult pushed to Viewers who block XXX |

## Distinguishability standard

If a reasonable Viewer could treat it as a real person in ordinary use, it is prohibited. Animation does not get a free pass if it is passable as real humanity.

## Enforcement

Policy absolute. Community mute/report/block (doc 17). IA of IA private inquiry + conduct steering (doc 17). Perfect detection not claimed.
