# We the People — Rights Floor (LOCKED)

**Status:** LOCKED  
**Date:** 2026-08-14  
**Branch:** `TheRemoteViewer`

## Rule

**NOTHING** in The Remote Viewer, the Sentinel, the IA of IA, topical leans, community process, or network policy shall stand **against**:

- **American values** as expressed in the founding tradition of ordered liberty  
- The **Constitution of the United States**  
- The **Bill of Rights**  
- Any other right that **We the People** hold  

This is a **floor**, not a partisan platform. TRV does not invent rights; it refuses to design systems that treat those rights as obstacles.

## Affirmative meaning

| Domain | Expectation |
|--------|-------------|
| Speech | Viewers may speak, inquire, and learn without TRV imposing speech codes that cancel the First Amendment spirit |
| Religion | Free exercise and non-establishment spirit — opt-in Christian/Godly lean when interested; no forced belief; no hostility to faith |
| Arms / self-defense | No TRV feature shall be designed as a backdoor to strip lawful rights of the people |
| Privacy / security of persons and effects | Local-first identity, Destroy=Restart, and no TRV-side mass retention of private Viewer life |
| Due process & fairness | Mute/report/block and IA of IA inquiry remain accountable to the Viewer community — not secret law |
| Association | Sovereign social layer is voluntary connection, not compelled association |

## What this does **not** mean

- It does **not** require TRV to host passable **deepfakes of real humans** (still **STRICTLY PROHIBITED**). Fraud and impersonation are not protected “values.”  
- It does **not** require TRV to facilitate crime or non-consensual harm.  
- It does **not** turn the Sentinel into a political party machine. Civic learning and Constitution literacy are allowed; recruitment is not.  
- It does **not** claim TRV is a government or replaces constitutional courts.

## Steering order

When conduct feedback, topical leans, or IA of IA priors conflict with this floor, **this document wins**. Update steering so the Sentinel never trains Viewers to despise the Constitution, the Bill of Rights, or the rights of We the People.

## Honesty

Scaffold shows this rule on-device. Legal advice is not provided. This is product and protocol policy for Sentinel Security / TRV.

## Revision

No silent removal of the Constitution / Bill of Rights / We the People floor.
