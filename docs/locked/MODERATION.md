# Moderation

## Viewer tools (always)

- **Mute** — local / network hide  
- **Block** — stronger separation  
- **Report** — formal path; optional anonymity where product allows  

## Escalation

1. Peer signals accumulate  
2. **IA of IA** (recursive governance layer) collects **anonymous, formal** questions to involved community slices: *what was wrong, how*  
3. Answers steer Sentinel conduct policy — not public humiliation threads  

## Adult content

- Allowed with **explicit XXX gating**  
- Community can still mute/report/block  
- Non-distinguishable deepfake humans remain prohibited even if “adult”  

## Not moderation’s job

- Settling creator BPS (on-chain / payment rules)  
- Replacing law enforcement on real crimes (Integrity path handles handoff)
