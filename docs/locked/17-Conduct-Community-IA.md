## 1. Animations of a human

**Any animation made of a human is OK** when it remains **distinguishable from humanity** (see locked doc 16).

- Stylized, drawn, 3D-rigged, cartoon, or clearly synthetic human animation is allowed.  
- It must not pass as a real live human or impersonate a real person.

## 2. Adult content and XXX blocking

Adult content is **permitted** on TRV rails only behind **XXX blocking**:

- Viewers must **opt in** to see XXX-marked material.  
- Default for new Viewers: **XXX blocked** (adult hidden).  
- Creators who publish adult animation or adult text/voice must mark it **XXX** so blockers never receive it unfiltered.  
- XXX is a **Viewer preference + content label**, not a moral claim by TRV about adults’ private choices.

Deepfakes / indistinguishable real-human synthesis remain **STRICTLY PROHIBITED** even if marked XXX.

## 3. Community tools — mute, report, block

Any Viewer may, toward any other Viewer who has **potentially gone too far**:

| Action | Effect |
|--------|--------|
| **Mute** | Stop seeing their posts/messages in local feeds; reversible by muter |
| **Report** | File a local + (when live) network report with optional short reason |
| **Block** | Hard local cut: no messages, no connection surface from that DID on this device |

These tools are **Viewer-controlled first**. They do not require waiting for IA.

## 4. IA of IA — private community inquiry

When reports accumulate or severity warrants, the **IA of IA** (recursive governance layer for the Sentinel) shall:

1. Convene an internal assessment (when Stage D / IA-of-IA is live; until then, **scaffold drafts only**).  
2. Find an **anonymous yet formal** way to ask the **community in private** what was wrong and how.  
3. Send that inquiry without exposing the identity of reporters or the target beyond what process requires.  
4. Collect structured replies: *what was wrong* · *how it harmed* · *what boundary was crossed*.

## 5. Steering Sentinel conduct

Once private community feedback is received, the **IA of IA uses that feedback** (as well as locked rules 15–17 and 16) to **steer how the Sentinel should go with conduct**:

- Update conduct priors / refusal patterns for the on-device and network Sentinel.  
- Prefer human community signal over silent corporate moderation theater.  
- Never use this loop to authorize deepfakes of real humans or indistinguishable passable synthesis.
