# Creator living wage path (product policy)

**Intent:** A Viewer who creates consistently on TRV can **make a living** from that work — not only hobby tips.

## Economic rules already locked

| Sale type | Creator | Pool | Platform |
|-----------|---------|------|----------|
| Digital content | **95%** | 5% | **0%** |
| NFT primary (TRV-minted) | **90%** | 10% | **0%** |
| NFT secondary | **5%** royalty to creator | 0% | 0% |

Platform does **not** skim a second cut. Pool is public goods, not “house edge.”

## What “make a living” requires (honest)

| Need | TRV posture |
|------|-------------|
| **Keep almost all revenue** | 95/90% — yes |
| **Own audience** | Creator can export list / point off-platform; no hostage followers |
| **Reliable payout** | Clear settlement (USDC/SOL/fiat off-ramp) on a published schedule |
| **Discovery** | Signal tiers boost sub/node; free can still sell (weaker signal) |
| **Volume** | Product must reach buyers — living wage is **not guaranteed** by split alone |
| **Low fees elsewhere** | Prefer chain + direct pay; avoid forced app-store 30% where possible (Graphene / web / Obtainium paths) |

Split math makes a living *possible*. Distribution and quality make it *real*.

## Product commitments

1. **No surprise platform fee** on creator sales (0%).  
2. **Payout transparency** — creator sees gross, pool bps, net.  
3. **Exclusivity optional** — never force all-or-nothing that traps income.  
4. **Tools to sell** — listings, NFT mint path, digital delivery (TRVL/age patterns where they fit).  
5. **Do not tax unlimited-comms entitlement** as a second rent on creators who already sub or node.  
6. **Secondary royalty** stays with **original creator** (5% default).

## What TRV will not claim

- “Everyone gets rich”  
- Guaranteed monthly minimum without a real buyer base  
- That weak-signal free tier alone equals full-time income  

## Ops targets (when store is live)

- Settlement ≤ **7 days** after confirmed payment (or instant on-chain)  
- Creator dashboard: sales, refunds, pool share, rank/signal effect  
- Dispute path that does not strand funds for months  

## Relation to nodes

Running a node earns **unlimited comms**, not automatic creator revenue.  
Creators who also node get **signal** advantages — still sell with their own work.

*SCAFFOLD policy. Living wage = high creator share + real distribution + honest marketing.*
