## Corrections before citation

1. Operation Mockingbird. The Church Committee documented CIA relationships with journalists and media organizations. A single formal program under the name Operation Mockingbird is not established in the declassified record the way CORONA, Venona, or COINTELPRO are. Treat the name as a secondary label.
2. XKeyscore. Public disclosure was 2013. The 2008–2013 window in the source catalog is a disclosure window, not a confirmed end date.
3. Stargate. CIA released the remote-viewing records in 1995. The American Institutes for Research evaluation did not find operational value sufficient to continue the program. Precursor unit names below are real. That record is not a product claim.
4. AATIP / AAWSAP. The Pentagon program existed. The catalog states the tasking. It does not establish an extraterrestrial finding.
5. Satellite pairings marked below as supplied (FARRAH with JUMPSEAT, Rainbow with Gusto) need an NRO or CIA declassification release before a seat cites the pairing as locked.

## Catalog as supplied

Agencies covered: CIA, NSA, FBI, NRO, DIA/DOD.
Fields kept from the source: primary codename, alternate designations, timeline, mission line.

### CIA — HUMINT / TECHINT

- MKUltra (1953–1973). Alt: MKDELTA, MKNAOMI, CHATTER, BLUEBIRD, ARTICHOKE. Human behavioral-modification research: psychoactive drugs including LSD, and psychological interrogation methods. Officially acknowledged; widely documented as abusive.
- Project CORONA (1958–1972). Alt: Keyhole KH-1 through KH-4, Discoverer. First operational U.S. photographic reconnaissance satellite. Film capsules recovered in mid-air.
- Project ARGON and Project LANYARD (1961–1963). Alt: KH-5 ARGON, KH-6 LANYARD. ARGON for mapping imagery. LANYARD for high-resolution target acquisition.
- Project OXCART (1957–1968). Alt: Lockheed A-12, Cygnus, Archangel. Mach 3+ high-altitude reconnaissance aircraft. Technological predecessor of the USAF SR-71.
- Project Azorian (1968–1974). Alt: Project Jennifer, Hughes Glomar Explorer. Covert attempt to raise the sunken Soviet ballistic-missile submarine K-129. Partial recovery is the public record.
- Operation Mockingbird (c. 1950–1976). Alt in source: CIA Office of Special Projects media network. See correction 1 before citing the codename.
- Operation Paperclip (1945–1959). Alt: Operation Overcast. Transfer of German scientists, engineers, and technicians, including Wernher von Braun, to U.S. programs. Source figure: over 1,600 people.
- Stargate Project (1978–1995). Alt: GONDOLA WISH, GRILL FLAME, CENTER LANE, SUN STREAK, SCANATE, Project CF. Remote-viewing research for military and intelligence use. See correction 3.
- Project Rainbow and Project Gusto (1957–1959). Alt in source: Project AQUATONE, Skunk Works stealth precursors. Radar-cross-section reduction for high-altitude aircraft (U-2 and A-12). Pairing needs primary confirmation.

### NSA — SIGINT / CYBER

- Venona Project (1943–1980). Alt: BRIDE, DRUG, LACE. Decryption of Soviet one-time-pad traffic reused in error. Exposed Soviet espionage networks in North America.
- Project MINARET and Project SHAMROCK (1945–1975). Alt in source: Industry Intercept Program, Domestic Security Watch List. Watch-list monitoring of international communications of U.S. persons, and bulk telegraphic interception with carrier cooperation. Church Committee record.
- Trailblazer and ThinThread (1998​–2006). Alt in source: NSA Digital Network Intelligence Modernization / Project P-238. Mass-traffic analysis prototypes. ThinThread included privacy controls that were not carried into the deployed path. Trailblazer was the failed modernization.
- XKeyscore (2008–2013 in source). Alt: DNI Exploitation System. Query system over collected signals intelligence. See correction 2.

### FBI — counterintelligence / law enforcement

- COINTELPRO (1956–1971). Alt: Counterintelligence Program, Operation Hoodwink. Covert disruption of domestic political organizations. Later found improper and, in significant parts, illegal.
- Operation Solo (1952–1980). Alt: Agent NY 694-S*, CPUSA infiltration. Morris Childs as a long-running informant inside Communist Party USA leadership and in contact with Soviet counterparts.
- Operation Ghost Stories (2000–2010). Alt: Russian Illegals Program surveillance. FBI counterespionage case against deep-cover SVR officers. Arrests announced in 2010.

### NRO — GEOINT / reconnaissance

- GAMBIT (1963–1984). Alt: KH-7 and KH-8. High-resolution film-return imagery satellites.
- HEXAGON (1971–1986). Alt: KH-9, Big Bird. Wide-area search film-return system. Multiple re-entry vehicles. Source length: 55 feet.
- Manned Orbiting Laboratory (1963–1969). Alt: Project DORIAN, KH-10. Cancelled crewed reconnaissance-station program.
- POPPY and PARCAE (1962–1987). Alt: GRAB, White Cloud, NOSS. Ocean surveillance electronic-intelligence constellations.
- FARRAH and JUMPSEAT (1971–1983). Alt in source: AFP-711, Chalet, Vortex. Geosynchronous and Molniya SIGINT collection. Pairing needs an NRO release before citation.

### DIA / DOD — defense and advanced research

- Project Sun Streak / Grill Flame (1977–1995). Alt: DIA psychoenergetics program, Detachment 45. Military remote-viewing units later folded into Stargate.
- AATIP (2007–2012). Alt: AAWSAP. Unpublicized Pentagon review of unidentified aerial phenomena and anomalous sensor encounters. See correction 4.
- Project Sunshine (1953–1959). Alt: AEC/DOD strontium-90 fallout study. Measurement of strontium-90 in human tissue and bone after atmospheric nuclear tests. Collection included remains obtained without consent.
- Project Iceworm and Project Horizon (1959–1966). Alt: Camp Century (Iceworm), U.S. Army Military Lunar Outpost (Horizon). Iceworm: covert sub-ice missile concept in Greenland, not fielded as designed. Horizon: 1959 study for a military lunar outpost, not built.
